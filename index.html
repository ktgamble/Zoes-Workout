Warm-up

About a minute each.

  • Arm circles and swings
  • Incline scap push-ups
  • Light cable face pulls
  • Dead hang or scap pull-ups (switch to a lat stretch from the 2nd trimester)
5:003 min

Push-up primer

5 rounds of 2 strict push-ups, about 30 seconds rest between rounds.

Quality over quantity. If 2 strict reps aren't there today, raise the hands slightly.

8:0019 min

Lifting rounds

Each round is a tri-set: heavy, medium, endurance back to back with 20–30 seconds between, then 45–60 seconds rest. Do 2 sets.

Heavy means 2–3 reps left in the tank. No grinding, no breath-holding. If she needs more rest, drop a set rather than rush.

27:003 min

Transition

30–40 seconds each, to mild tension only. Then water and set up the mat.

  • Chest stretch in a doorway or on the cable frame
  • Lat stretch holding the pull-up bar
  • Cross-body shoulder stretch, each side
  • Overhead triceps stretch, each side
  • Wide-knee child's pose

Core and stability circuit

3 rounds. 40 seconds work, 20 seconds rest at each station.

StationWork
